Family Budget AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Family Budget AI helps individuals and families organize budgets, track income and expenses, and manage shared household financial information.

1. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the app may process budget entries, income, expenses, categories, household information, and information entered by users for sharing with family members.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide budgeting features, display financial summaries, and support family budget collaboration where available.

3. Family Sharing

When a user chooses to share budget information with family members, authorized participants may be able to view or edit the shared information according to the app's available permissions. Users should share information only with people they trust.

4. Storage and Security

The storage, transmission, and protection of information depend on the app's implemented functionality. Users should avoid entering passwords, bank card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information into budget descriptions.

5. Third-Party Services

If the app uses external hosting, analytics, authentication, advertising, or AI services, relevant information may be processed by those providers to deliver the requested functionality. Any such processing must be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration.

6. User Choices and Deletion

Users may request information about the handling or deletion of their personal data by contacting the developer. Available deletion options depend on the app's implemented storage and account features.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically designed for children. Parents and guardians should supervise any family sharing involving minors.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
